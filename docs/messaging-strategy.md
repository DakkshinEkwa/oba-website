# OBA Website Messaging Strategy & Copy Rewrite

Prepared for the Ophthalmology Business Academy (obacademy.org) rebuild in this repository.
Scope: full messaging audit, repositioning, and publication-ready copy — implemented in the site source alongside this document.

---

## 1. Executive Diagnosis

The current website is a well-built content site with a positioning problem it cannot outgrow by polishing sentences:

1. **It reads as a lead-generation funnel wearing an academy brand.** The single site-wide macro-conversion — in the header, the homepage hero, and the closing band of every major page — is "Analyze Your Marketing," a marketing-services offer. Whatever the education says, the architecture says *this site exists to sell you a marketing analysis*. That directly undermines the one thing OBA must own: credible, non-promotional professional education.

2. **The hero sells a product that doesn't exist.** "Personalized business education" describes nothing on the site — there is no personalization, no curriculum, no training program. What OBA actually has is genuinely valuable and undersold: 75 recorded conversations with named operators and physicians about real practice decisions.

3. **It makes unsupported claims where it has real proof, and real claims where it needs none.** "Join 1,000s of practice owners" and "Join thousands of ophthalmology practices" appear with no supporting data, while the site's actual, verifiable assets (75 episodes since 2022, six named hosts with operator credentials, a fully ophthalmology-specific catalog) are buried in a stat band. Fabricated-sounding social proof next to real proof lowers the credibility of both.

4. **The growth engine has no front door.** OBA's model depends on attracting credible speakers and appropriate industry partners. The site has *no speaker page, no partnership page, and no way to express interest in either* — the contact page mentions "partnering" in passing. The audiences that matter most strategically have no journey at all.

5. **Promises outrun the programming.** "New content every week," "weekly strategies," "on-demand webinars," and "live events" are promised across membership, newsletter, and footer copy — while the webinar archive and events pages are empty and the episode catalog's most recent entry is June 2024. Every overpromise a visitor can disprove in one click costs more trust than it earns.

6. **Everything is one undifferentiated pitch.** Practice owners, prospective speakers, industry partners, and emerging leaders all see the same "grow your practice" message. Nothing on the site tells a respected ophthalmologist why contributing would be worthy of their reputation, or tells an industry executive that education here isn't for sale.

**The one-sentence version:** the site currently positions OBA as *a free content library that funnels ophthalmologists toward a marketing-services offer*; it should position OBA as *the professional platform where experienced ophthalmology leaders examine, on the record, the business decisions behind stronger practices*.

### The three most important messaging changes

1. **Re-center the site on experience-led conversation, not content volume or vendor conversion.** The product is the quality of the people and the candor of the discussion — lead with that; demote the marketing-analysis offer to a clearly labeled, transparently attributed service.
2. **Build the contributor and partner journeys.** Add a speaker page and a partnership page with real editorial standards, a clear format, and low-friction expression-of-interest actions — and make "Contribute as a Speaker" the site's primary CTA.
3. **Replace every unsupported claim with the real proof already on hand.** Drop "thousands," drop "weekly" where cadence isn't guaranteed, describe empty sections honestly, and put the verifiable numbers (75+ conversations, six named hosts, since 2022, 100% ophthalmology) to work.

## 3. Core Positioning Recommendation

### One-sentence positioning statement (internal)

The Ophthalmology Business Academy convenes experienced ophthalmologists, practice leaders, and industry experts for candid, non-promotional conversations — podcasts, panels, and webinars — that help ophthalmology practices navigate the business decisions clinical training never covered, differently from CME providers and vendor events because every conversation is led by firsthand experience and kept free of sales agendas.

### Primary website value proposition (five-second test)

**The business of ophthalmology, discussed by the people who run it.**

### Supporting value proposition

OBA brings together practice owners, administrators, physicians, and industry experts for candid conversations about growth, operations, patient experience, leadership, and technology — 75+ recorded conversations since 2022, all specific to eye care, none of them sales pitches.

### Brand promise

Every conversation OBA publishes is led by someone with firsthand experience of the problem, is framed around real decisions and trade-offs, and is kept separate from anyone's commercial agenda.

### Messaging pillars

**Pillar 1 — Experience-led, not theory-led.**
Core message: the people talking have actually made these decisions.
Support: hosts and contributors run practices (Mann Eye Institute's COO, practice-owner physicians, a certified ophthalmic executive); guests are drawn from operators, clinicians, and implementers.
Evidence required: keep host bios verifiable; surface named guests per episode (currently only in show notes — see §7).
Primary audiences: ophthalmologists & practice leaders; prospective speakers.

**Pillar 2 — Specific to ophthalmology.**
Core message: generic business advice breaks on the realities of an eye-care practice; OBA's conversations start from those realities.
Support: 100% of the catalog is ophthalmology-specific — premium conversion, co-management compliance, office-based surgery, subspecialty practice building, private equity in eye care.
Evidence required: none beyond the catalog itself; keep episode framing concrete.
Primary audiences: ophthalmologists & practice leaders; emerging leaders.

**Pillar 3 — A credible stage for contributors.**
Core message: participating strengthens the professional ideas associated with your name — a well-prepared, professionally produced conversation about a problem you understand deeply, never a promotional booking.
Support: guided preparation, professional production, published editorial standards.
Evidence required: speaker page must describe the real process; collect speaker testimonials over time (§7).
Primary audience: prospective speakers and contributors.

**Pillar 4 — Education kept separate from promotion.**
Core message: commercial partners support the conversation; they don't script it. OBA discloses commercial relationships plainly — starting with its own founding relationship to Ekwa Marketing.
Support: published partnership standards; transparent labeling of the complimentary marketing-analysis service.
Evidence required: the standards themselves, applied consistently.
Primary audiences: industry partners; skeptical physicians.

### Differentiation statement

- **Vs. clinical education platforms and associations:** OBA doesn't teach medicine and doesn't compete for CME. It covers what those institutions structurally can't: the business, leadership, and operational decisions of running a practice, discussed candidly by people who've made them.
- **Vs. generic business webinars and practice-management blogs:** OBA's conversations start from ophthalmology's specifics — its referral patterns, its payer realities, its surgical service lines — not from business advice with an eye-care logo on it.
- **Vs. vendor-led events and promotional interview platforms:** at OBA, nobody's participation is a purchase. Speakers are invited for experience, not sponsorship; partners support conversations they don't control; and the one commercial service connected to OBA (Ekwa's complimentary marketing analysis) is labeled as exactly that.

## 5. Rewritten Website Copy

The full publication-ready copy is implemented directly in the site source; this section records it page by page. (Where a line is annotated ⚑, see §7 Claims & Proof.)

- **Header CTA:** Contribute as a Speaker → `/speak`
- **Footer newsletter band:** H: "Insight for ophthalmology practice leaders" / B: "New conversations, practical articles, and event invitations — straight to your inbox." (drops the unverifiable "weekly")
- **Footer columns:** Academy column now: About · Become a Speaker · Partnerships · Membership · Marketing Analysis · Contact

### 5.2 Home `/`

- Purpose: answer what/for whom/why trust/how to engage, in that order. Primary audience: all four. Primary CTA: Browse the conversations. Secondary: Contribute as a Speaker.
- **SEO title:** default. **Meta:** site description.
- **Hero eyebrow:** "The Ophthalmology Business Academy"
- **Hero H1:** "The business of ophthalmology, / *discussed by the people who run it.*"
- **Hero copy:** "Candid, non-promotional conversations with the practice owners, administrators, physicians, and industry experts navigating the decisions behind stronger eye-care practices."
- **CTAs:** [Browse the Conversations → /podcast/episodes] [Contribute as a Speaker → /speak]
- **Micro-labels:** Experience-led / Hosted by operators and physicians · Ophthalmology-specific / Built on eye-care realities · Non-promotional / Conversations, not sales pitches
- **Featured strip:** eyebrow "Latest conversation" (unchanged mechanics)
- **Stat band:** 75+ Recorded conversations · 6 Hosts & regular contributors · 2022 Convening leaders since · 100% Ophthalmology-specific
- **Section — The problems we examine:** eyebrow "What we talk about"; H2 "The decisions that determine / *whether a practice thrives.*"; lede "Clinical training builds excellent physicians. It rarely covers the decisions that follow — and those decisions are what OBA's conversations are for." Four columns: Growth & referrals; Patient experience & care; Leadership & teams; Technology & innovation (full copy in source).
- **Section — Who it's for:** four audience blocks (Practice owners & partners; Administrators & operations leaders; Industry & technology leaders; Emerging leaders).
- **Section — Ways to engage:** Listen & apply → episodes; Contribute your experience → /speak; Support the conversation → /partnerships.
- Latest episodes and blog sections retained; closing CTA band = speaker CTA (site default).

### 5.3 About `/about`

- **SEO title:** "About OBA". **Meta:** "What the Ophthalmology Business Academy is, why it exists, and the editorial principles behind every conversation we publish."
- **Hero:** eyebrow "About OBA"; H1 "A forum for the decisions clinical training never covered"; lede "OBA convenes experienced ophthalmology leaders to examine, on the record, the business of running a stronger practice."
- **Intro:** the gap OBA exists to close; what it actually is (75+ conversations since 2022, who takes part); founding transparency — Naren Arulrajah / Ekwa Marketing disclosure with plain-language separation of education from Ekwa's complimentary analysis service. ⚑ (confirm phrasing with founder)
- **Principles (4):** Experience over theory · Decisions, not tips · Specific to ophthalmology · Education, not promotion.
- Hosts section retained. Closing CTA = speaker.

### 5.4 Become a Speaker `/speak` (new)

Copy implemented in full: why OBA invites experienced professionals; who we invite (five expertise groups spanning clinicians, owners/administrators, educators, patient-experience leaders, industry/technology leaders); what a strong conversation looks like (problem → decision → trade-offs → outcome); what to expect before/during/after (topic shaped together, guided preparation, remote professional recording, produced assets to share); the editorial standard (no product pitches, framing around problems not products, protecting the contributor's name); expression-of-interest form. Primary CTA: "Share Your Area of Expertise."

### 5.5 Partnerships `/partnerships` (new)

Copy implemented in full: who partners with OBA (diagnostics, imaging, surgical technology, AI, remote monitoring, workflow, patient engagement, therapeutics, vision rehabilitation, professional services); the standards that govern collaboration (education first; speakers selected for experience; partners never script the conversation; commercial support disclosed); what partnership can look like (framed as possibilities to discuss, not existing programs); enquiry form. Primary CTA: "Start a Partnership Conversation." No promises of leads, reach, access, or endorsement.

### 5.6 Podcast pages

- `/podcast` hero: "Where ophthalmology talks business, on the record" + reframed feature cards (Experience-led / Decision-focused / Non-promotional); closing CTA = speaker.
- `/podcast/episodes` hero lede sharpened; `/podcast/hosts` lede sharpened. Episode bodies (75 MDX files) intentionally untouched — see framing template below for future episodes/events.

**Episode & event framing template (for future listings):** recognizable problem + meaningful tension + desirable outcome. State (a) the real problem examined, (b) why it matters now, (c) the tension that makes it worth discussing, (d) who benefits from attending/listening, (e) the practical questions explored, (f) why these speakers are the right people, and (g) what the session will not be (a product demo, a generic lecture).

### 5.7 Resources, Newsletter, Events, Webinars

- Hub descriptions rewritten without cadence promises; webinars/events empty states rewritten honestly ("No events are currently scheduled — newsletter subscribers hear about new panels first" / archive framed as in development, podcast offered as the live body of work).
- Newsletter page: "thousands" removed; benefits restated to what's deliverable.

### 5.8 Membership `/membership`

Free membership restated honestly: full podcast library, articles, newsletter, invitations to future panels and events. Removed: "new educational content every week," "on-demand webinars" (none exist), "networking" (no community feature exists). $0 pricing and FAQ retained, reworded.

### 5.9 Contact `/contact`

Intent routing added: speaking → /speak, partnerships → /partnerships, everything else → form. Address/email retained. ⚑ (address is Ekwa's Mississauga office; confirm it's the right public address for OBA)

### 5.10 Marketing Analysis `/msm`

Reframed with full attribution: the analysis is provided by **Ekwa Marketing**, the practice-marketing firm led by OBA's founder ⚑ (confirm attribution wording); copy separates it explicitly from OBA's editorial content ("This is a service, not part of OBA's educational programming"). FAQ updated to answer "Who actually performs this?" honestly.

### 5.11 Auth pages

Auth pages (`/login`, `/register`, `/forgot-password`) and the account-based AuthShell/register/login forms have been **removed** from the site — there is no account system. Membership is free and account-free: the newsletter is the soft-conversion CTA on `/membership` (→ `/resources/newsletter`).

## 7. Claims & Proof Requirements

**Removed as unsupported (do not restore without data):**
- "Join 1,000s of practice owners" (homepage hero) · "Join thousands of practice owners" (newsletter) · "Join thousands of ophthalmology practices" (auth shell)
- "New business insight weekly" / "new educational content every week" / "weekly strategies" — cadence claims contradicted by the catalog (latest episode June 2024)
- Membership promises of "on-demand webinars" and "networking" — neither currently exists
- "Actionable in days, not months" (analyze page) — replaced with the factual turnaround description

**Claims retained that require standing evidence:**
- Host bios: "200-person team," "scale from startups to multi-million-dollar practices" (Naren), "practice more than doubled in size" (Guido) — plausible and attributed, but they are the site's only named-person claims; keep them accurate with the individuals' sign-off. "Dominate Google" reads as vendor language inside an education brand — recommend softening in `hosts.json` at next review (content file intentionally not rewritten here).
- "75+ recorded conversations," "since 2022," "six hosts" — auto-derived or verifiable; safe.

**Proof worth collecting (would materially strengthen the site):**
1. Named guest list per episode (the schema has a `guests` field; it's empty — populating it turns 75 episodes into a visible roster of contributors, the single strongest speaker-attraction asset available).
2. Speaker testimonials — two lines from past guests on what participating was like.
3. Audience numbers (downloads, subscribers, members) — only if real and specific.
4. Event/webinar outcomes once programming resumes — attendance, questions asked, follow-on discussion.
5. Partner acknowledgments, once real partners exist and standards are applied.

**Operational flags (not copy, but publish-blockers):**
- Social links in `site.ts` are bare placeholder domains — link real profiles or remove the icons.
- The Mississauga address is Ekwa's office — confirm it's OBA's intended public address.
- All forms (newsletter, contact, speaker, partnership, analyze, auth) are stubbed client-side demos — connect them before promoting the new conversion paths.
- Founding/Ekwa disclosure wording on /about and /msm needs the founder's sign-off.

## 8. Before-and-After Summary

**Before:** a content-marketing site. Generic mastery promise ("Master the business behind the practice"), unsupported social proof, one vendor-serving CTA repeated everywhere, no path for the two audiences OBA's model actually depends on (speakers and partners), and programming promises the empty pages immediately contradict.

**After:** a professional forum. The site leads with what is verifiably true and genuinely differentiating — experienced, named people discussing real decisions in ophthalmology, without sales agendas. Each of the four audiences has a visible journey and a specific next action; participation (speaking, partnering) is the site's primary conversion; the commercial service that funds the platform's origins is disclosed and contained rather than disguised as the destination. Every claim on the site can survive a click.

- **No unsupported claims** — "thousands," "weekly," phantom webinars/networking removed; remaining claims verifiable or flagged in §7. ✔
- **Reflects practice-leadership realities** — copy is organized around decisions and trade-offs (referrals, staffing, technology adoption, patient experience, succession/PE) rather than abstract "growth." ✔
- **Distinct from generic education and media platforms** — differentiation stated explicitly on /about and enacted structurally (editorial standards, disclosure, participation model). ✔
