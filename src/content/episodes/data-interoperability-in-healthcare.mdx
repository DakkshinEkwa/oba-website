Data interoperability in healthcare refers to the seamless exchange and use of patient information among different systems and healthcare providers to improve care coordination and decision-making.

In this episode, Anita Nayak explores how data interoperability is integrated into Ophthalmology, influencing patient care and clinical decision-making.

Anita Nayak is the Chief Executive Officer of ClinDCast LLC, a leading provider of Healthcare technology solutions and consulting services.

With over 15 years of experience in healthcare delivery, she has gained a deep understanding of the challenges faced by healthcare organizations. Her track record showcases a proven ability to deliver innovative solutions that improve patient outcomes and streamline operations through various EHR implementations, including Epic, Cerner, Athena, Onco EMR, NextGen and eCW

As a passionate advocate for improving healthcare through technology, Ms. Nayak is committed to assisting her clients in staying ahead of the curve in terms of innovation and realizing their full potential. Her areas of expertise encompass interoperability solutions, FHIR, HL7, CCDA and TEFCA, making her drive digital transformation in the healthcare sector.
