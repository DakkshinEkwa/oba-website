In this episode, Kathleen A. Otto, Director of Marketing at Fitchte, Endl & Elmer Eyecare, talks about the “Cataract Surgery Lunch and Learn.” The cataract surgery lunch and learn is a thoughtful, safe event with IN-PERSON information delivery. Kathleen has implemented result-driven marketing strategies to attract patients to this event and increased the number of cataract surgeries performed in her practice through this strategy.

Kathy Otto has over 20 years of experience in Ophthalmology & currently serves as the Director of Marketing at Fichte, Endl & Elmer Eyecare in Buffalo NY. She holds an A.A.S. in Surgical Technology and is a Certified Ophthalmic Assistant. She used her clinical and O.R. experience to transition into numerous roles including Practice Liaison for an OD referral network, Community Outreach Coordinator, and Coordinator of Marketing & Outreach.

Kathy is regarded as an industry thought leader in the field of Ophthalmology Practice Marketing. She has spoken at International Ophthalmology conferences including the American Association of Cataract & Refractive Surgeons (ASCRS), the American Society of Ophthalmic Administrators (ASOA) & American Medical Optics University (AMOU). She has been featured on medical marketing webinars put on by Glacial Multimedia & a featured speaker on the ZEISS SMILE users group podcast. On a local level, she has lectured to her peers at the annual Association of Technical Personnel in Ophthalmology (ATPO) meeting in Western NY.
