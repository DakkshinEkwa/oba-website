In this episode, Alexa Montesino, the managing partner of iDocSocial, collaborates with Sarah Duval to explore the vision behind their innovative startup, iDocSocial, and discuss their future plans for the development of the ophthalmology landscape.

A Miami, Florida native, Alexa received her Bachelors of Science degree from the University of Florida and her Masters in Business Administration from Nova Southeastern University. She later went on to receive a Certificate of Analytics from the Wharton School of Business.

Alexa is highly attuned to organizational ideals and strives to balance a spirit of fun and positivity with innovative strategy. A strong believer in connectivity, Alexa believes in long lasting relationships and aims to find new solutions to any problem. With strong networking skills and emotional intelligence, physicians and commercial partners can achieve results.
