In this episode, Chris Bonsall, Vice President of Revenue Generation at Navigate Patient Solutions, explores the vital role of human interaction in patient pre-education. He addresses the limitations of traditional non-human patient education methods and emphasizes the inefficacy of these approaches. Chris underscores the significance of personalized, one-on-one communication with patients, particularly in increasing conversions to premium ophthalmic surgical options.

Vice President of Revenue GenerationNavigate Patient Solutions

Chris Bonsall has spent over a decade working with Ophthalmology practices to optimize their clinic flow by implementing the right software solutions. Through consulting with hundreds of practices across the US, he realized that the most efficient, profitable practices were adopting software as needed but were investing more heavily in patient experience.

Chris is now the Vice President of Revenue Generation for Navigate Patient Solutions, a unique company focused on bringing the “Human Approach” back to patient education for cataract patients. He is passionate about helping practices improve their education process to ensure no patient goes through cataract surgery without knowing the options available to them.
