Today’s episode features Mike Guelcher, managing partner and co-founder at Prism Consulting Partners. Mike brings his leadership and organizational development expertise to provide valuable insights into the significance of effective leadership in the post-pandemic world. He shares actionable strategies to enhance leadership within your practice, offering deep insights into the evolving landscape of employee retention and the rise of the Great Resignation phenomenon.
