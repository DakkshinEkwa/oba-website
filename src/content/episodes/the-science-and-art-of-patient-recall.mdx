In this episode of the Ophthalmology Business Podcast, host Guido Piquet is having an insightful conversation with Steve Gottfredson, owner of Brevium, about mastering patient recall. They discuss the fundamentals and benefits of a structured recall system. Steve also shares practical strategies learned from his life experiences to optimize recall campaigns, including technology, personalization, and multi-channel communication. By the end, you’ll have actionable strategies to improve patient recall campaigns in your ophthalmology practice.

Steve Gottfredson, is an owner and early employee at Brevium. He has over 15 years of experience working with ophthalmologists to improve patient retention. Steve’s company completed a large study about identifying what truly works for getting lost patients back on the schedule. He is eager to share insights on the real drivers of successful patient relationships in healthcare.
