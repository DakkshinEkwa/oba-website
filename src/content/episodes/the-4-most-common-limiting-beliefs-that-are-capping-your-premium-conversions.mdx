In this insightful episode, Troy Cole, the Catalyst-in-Chief at LogiCole Consulting, LLC, is trying to debunk the myths constraining your premium conversions. He explores four pervasive limiting beliefs that may be holding you back.

Troy discusses the latest trends in premium conversions, providing valuable insights into this constantly changing field.

Furthermore, he generously shares a curated list of resources to empower you in your quest to master premium conversions. This episode aims to challenge assumptions, explore opportunities, and pave the way for a more prosperous future in premium conversions.

Troy Cole is a seasoned professional with nearly 20 years of experience in ophthalmology. He began his career in marketing, working closely with elective surgeons, particularly in LASIK and cataract procedures. With a deep understanding of ophthalmological marketing, Troy founded LogiCole Consulting, LLC, seven years ago. Together with his wife, Susan, who brings her agency-side marketing and pharmaceutical sales experience, they provide comprehensive solutions for ophthalmology practices. Troy’s expertise lies in marketing strategy, communications, and sales within the eye care industry.
