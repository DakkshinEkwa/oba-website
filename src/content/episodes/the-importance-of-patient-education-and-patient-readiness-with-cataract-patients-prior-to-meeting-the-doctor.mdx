In this episode, Christine Lapointe, Director of Growth Programs at Eyecare Partners, LLC, discusses a crucial aspect of patient care in the field of ophthalmology: The significance of patient education and readiness among cataract patients before their initial consultation with the doctor.

Imagine this: your patients are armed with insights about their condition, possible treatments, and the process before they even set foot in the clinic. Picture yourself having a soothing conversation, guiding them through the details, and then seamlessly leading them into the surgical procedure. That’s where the magic of pre-education comes in, making the journey for your cataract patients much simpler.

So, let’s join Christine as she breaks down the ins and outs of pre-education. We’re covering everything from what it entails to why it matters.
