In this episode, we are joined by the accomplished Dr. Margot Goodkin, an inspiring medical professional who has navigated various career transitions with resilience and determination. Dr. Goodkin shares her experiences and valuable insights on embracing opportunities, overcoming challenges, and achieving success in different fields.

Board-Certified OphthalmologistPHD Holder in MicrobiologyLeader in Pharmaceutical Industry

She is a leader in the pharmaceutical industry and an ophthalmologist with advanced subspecialty training in the medical and surgical treatment of glaucoma. She also holds a PhD in Microbiology. Her experience and expertise span the disciplines of clinical and academic medicine; non-clinical research; government (FDA) service; and global development in the pharmaceutical industry. She is an active member of clinical and scientific research societies.
