Although many ophthalmologists and specialists get patients through word of mouth and referrals, an ever-increasing number of patients still go online to get more information.

We also have to consider the patients who didn’t get a referral from another doctor, friend, or family member, perhaps because they moved to a new city or didn’t know anyone with experience in your specialty. They are a smaller group of people but are also highly likely to search online.

To find an ophthalmologist nearby, patients will generally enter search keywords like “ophthalmologist near me” or “Physicians near me”, etc.

Our marketing team will spend 4 hours researching you and your competition online prior to this meeting to identify further opportunities on how Digital Marketing will help you grow your practice.
