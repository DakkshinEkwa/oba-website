As leaders/ ophthalmology practice owners, we are always looking for methods to sharpen our abilities and develop more effective team-leading techniques. However, have you ever thought about how your leadership style is influenced by emotional intelligence?

In this episode, Reputable HR consultant and leadership specialist Mike Lyons explores the potential of emotional intelligence and how it may change the way we influence and lead others in ophthalmology practices. He explains the four key elements of emotional intelligence and how you can use these elements for the benefit of your practice.

Mike is a seasoned HR leader who has worked in environments ranging from startup to Fortune 500. He spent five years as the Director of HR for a large retina practice and has been a frequent presenter at ASOA and AAOE conferences on the topics of leadership, culture, and employee engagement. He can be found on LinkedIn and Instagram at @MikeLyonsHR
